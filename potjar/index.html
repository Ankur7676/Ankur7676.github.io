Potjar

A calm, offline bill splitter for trips, flats and dinners. Split equally, by exact amounts, shares or percent, see who owes whom, and settle with the fewest payments.

  • No account and no ads
  • Works fully offline, with no internet permission
  • Your records stay on your phone; back them up to a file you choose
  • Share a summary with UPI and PayPal links
